What happened

The booking-v3 booking form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed the month first, as told, and the form read the date in the tenant’s day-first order. The swap happened in the form, before the parser saw the date, so the parser fix did not touch it.

Only form reports from day-first tenants on booking-v3: Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. Their app and email reports are parser.

How to recognise it

Intake can’t: it tags from the reporter’s browser, and many of these reporters use an en-US browser. Look at the tenant’s form (booking-v3) and the channel (form).
