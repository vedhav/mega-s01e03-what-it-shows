The three problems

parser: month-first parser. “Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.” Every day/month swap from a day-first tenant that did not come in through a booking-v3 form. Fixed by engineering: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3 date hint. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The form told people to type MM/DD/YYYY and then read the date day first. The parser fix did not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

one-day: one-day shift. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” No month is swapped, so neither fix touches it. It looks like a time zone or DST conversion. Still open, and under-reported: one reporter says they have stopped reporting these.

How to tell them apart

  1. A date one day off, not swapped? one-day, whatever the tenant (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove).
  2. A day-first tenant, through a booking-v3 form? hint.
  3. Any other day-first tenant? parser.

The subject line and intake’s tag decide nothing: every common subject appears in more than one problem, and intake tags from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant (triage shows the same with its examples).

For the desk

  • New date reports: a swap is no longer expected after 5 October. Treat one as a regression of the parser fix, and a one-day move as the open one-day ticket.
