What happened

No day and month swap: the date moves by one day.

Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.

Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.

Every booking in June is one day off. Only June.

The invite arrived before I made the booking.

Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.

It looks like a time zone or DST conversion between the tenant, the attendee and UTC. The desk read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), and the kb still files them under parser by tenant, so they are counted there as well.

How to recognise it

A date one day off, not swapped, from any tenant. The latest reporter (4 October) says they have stopped reporting these, so the count is low.
