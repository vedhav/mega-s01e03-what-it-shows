0002 · Duplicates share a cause, not a subject line

owner Vedha Viyash proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Replaces: 0001 once accepted

Context

Under 0001 the desk merged by subject line. #4471 took 412 reports under subjects such as “Wrong day on the invite” and was marked known, one fix queued. It held three problems (#4471 split); the queued fix closed one of them.

Decision

Two reports are the same issue when they share a cause (causes.ts, tried in triage): the tenant’s locale and booking form, the channel, and what the report says happened. The subject line is a search aid, not a reason to merge.

Consequences

  • Triage reads the body, not just the subject. Slower per report; far fewer tickets marked known while part of them is still broken.
  • A big ticket gets its cause table read before it is marked known.
  • 0001 stays as written; this record replaces it once accepted.
