How a report was placed

  1. Is it a one-day move? The desk read these reports as a date that moved by one day, not a swapped day and month (desk-notes.json). They go to one-day, whatever their tenant.
  2. Otherwise, by tenant (causes.ts): a day-first tenant’s report that came in through a booking-v3 form is hint; every other one is parser.

The subject line and intake’s tag decide nothing. Intake tags from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong both ways: some hint reports are tagged parser, and more parser reports are tagged hint.

The three problems

parser: month-first parser. “Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.” Every day and month swap that did not come through a booking-v3 form. One fix: parse dates in the tenant’s locale. This is the fix #4471 has queued.

hint: booking-v3 date hint. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The form tells people to type MM/DD/YYYY and then reads the date day first. The parser fix does not touch it: the form’s hint and its reading have to agree.

one-day: one-day shift. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” No month is swapped, so the parser fix does not touch it either. It looks like a time zone or DST conversion, and it needs its own investigation.
